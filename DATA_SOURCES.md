# Data sources

## 1. Cosmetics datasets (assets/cosmetics.csv)
- Author: Abid Ali Awan (kingabzpro), Kaggle
- https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kingabzpro/cosmetics-datasets
- License: GPL 2
- Used for: 1,438 products with ingredients and skin types declared by the brand

## 2. Sephora Products and Skincare Reviews (assets/sephora_products.csv, assets/community.csv)
- Author: Nady Inky (nadyinky), Kaggle
- https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nadyinky/sephora-products-and-skincare-reviews
- License: CC BY 4.0
- Changes: only skincare products; ingredient lists joined into plain text; 1,094,411 reviews
  summarized per product and skin type (number of reviews, average rating, % recommended,
  at least 3 reviews). Done in our Kaggle notebook.

## 3. Ingredient knowledge base (assets/ingredients.json)
- Written by the SkinCheck team, based on: Regulation (EC) 1223/2009 and amendments
  (2016/1198, 2021/1902, 2023/1545), SCCS opinion on parabens, Paula's Choice Ingredient
  Dictionary, INCIDecoder, American Academy of Dermatology. Sources are listed per ingredient.

SkinCheck gives general information, not medical advice.
